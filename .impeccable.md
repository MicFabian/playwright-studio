## Design Context

### Users

Primary users are QA engineers, SDETs, and developers who build and maintain Playwright automation. They use the product as an IDE-style workspace to assemble flows, manage reusable snippets, execute tests, and inspect run artifacts.

### Brand Personality

Pragmatic, technical, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a serious engineering tool: fast, clear, and predictable rather than playful or marketing-heavy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Desktop-first operator console with a canvas-first hierarchy, light workspace surfaces, and dark utility rails. Prefer high information density with clean visual grouping, and avoid noisy decorative patterns that compete with editing tasks.

### Design Principles

1. Prioritize canvas usability first: interactive controls and minimap must remain visible in initial viewports.
2. Keep interaction paths direct: click and drag workflows should both be supported for core actions.
3. Maintain clear hierarchy: strongest contrast and space go to active editing surfaces, not chrome.
4. Preserve file-backed trust: UI states should reflect persisted vs unsaved status clearly.
5. Optimize for frequent use: predictable layout, stable controls, minimal visual churn.
